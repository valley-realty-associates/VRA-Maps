    About dates

    Staunton was settled in 1732, its town plan was laid out in 1747, and it was incorporated in 1761. Many of the city’s oldest areas developed before modern subdivision platting, so some places do not have a single recorded subdivision-establishment date.

    Where available, the map uses verified plat, development, or building-era records.
